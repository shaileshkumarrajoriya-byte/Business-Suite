TEACHER PORTAL — USER GUIDE

Portal seedhe yahan se kholiye:

Open Teacher Portal
Step 1

Registration (Pehli Baar)

1
Teacher Portal kholiye (upar wale button se)
2
"New teacher? Register here" par tap kariye
3
Apna naam, mobile number, school (agar pata ho), aur kaunsi van mein aap travel karte hain — ye sab bharein
4
"Submit Registration" dabaiye
Registration bhejne ke baad office aapki details verify karega. Jab tak verify na ho, login nahi hoga — thoda intezaar karein.
Step 2

Login Kaise Karein

1
Teacher Portal kholiye
2
Apna 10 ankon ka mobile number daalein (wahi jo registration mein diya tha)
3
"Login" button dabaiye
What's inside

Portal Mein Kya-Kya Milega

Driver Ki Jaankari

Van chalane wale driver ka naam aur number — ek tap mein call kar sakte hain.

Van Ka Route Aur Time

Kis-kis stop se hoti hui van jayegi, aur har stop ka time — order mein.

Delay Alerts

Van late ho to driver ka bheja hua update aur wajah, turant dikhta hai.

Announcements

School ya office ki koi bhi jaroori suchna yahin dikhegi.

Optional

App Ko Phone Mein Install Karna

1
Portal ke upar-left corner mein download icon par tap kariye
2
Screen par diye steps follow kariye — Android par seedha install; iPhone par Share button se "Add to Home Screen"
Madad chahiye?

Login na ho paye, jaankari galat dikhe, ya kuch aur — office se seedhe sampark karein.
